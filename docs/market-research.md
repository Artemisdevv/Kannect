# Kerala retail and commerce landscape

**Desk research checked 11 October 2026.** This is secondary research, not a market-size estimate or evidence from shopkeeper interviews. Cite the date and scope of each figure; do not combine counts that describe different populations.

## Kerala retail indicators

| Indicator | Figure | What it measures and limits |
|---|---:|---|
| Shopping establishments | 5,774 | Kerala DES’s *Survey on Markets in Kerala: 2021–22* counts malls without supermarkets/hypermarkets, malls with them, supermarkets, and hypermarkets. It is not a census of every kirana, street vendor, or retail outlet. The survey report was signed 29 March 2025. [Kerala DES report, ch. 8, pp. 94–98](https://www.ecostat.kerala.gov.in/publication-detail/report-of-survey-on-markets-in-kerala-2021-22) |

| District distribution of the defined shopping establishments | Ernakulam 830; Thrissur 702; Kozhikode 700; Thiruvananthapuram 684; Idukki 91; Wayanad 45 | Counts share the same limited establishment definition above. [Kerala DES report, §8.1, p. 94](https://www.ecostat.kerala.gov.in/publication-detail/report-of-survey-on-markets-in-kerala-2021-22) |

**How to use these figures:** they support a narrow statement that Kerala has a large set of surveyed supermarket-format establishments and that few of those reported online-shopping facilities in the 2021–22 survey. They do not establish kirana-shop inventory practices, smartphone adoption, sales, or willingness to adopt Kannect. The Department of Industries and Commerce’s ONDC page describes seller onboarding steps and assistance; that is evidence of a state facilitation resource, not evidence that all eligible sellers are live on the network. [Kerala DIC: ONDC onboarding](https://www.industry.kerala.gov.in/index.php/open-network-for-digital-commerce-ondc)

## ONDC, DigiDukaan, and KiranaPro

### ONDC

ONDC is an interoperable commerce network, not a single shopping app. Its seller network participants connect sellers through seller applications, digitize catalogues, and handle seller-side functions such as payment disbursal. [ONDC: Seller Network Participants](https://www.ondc.org/pages/seller-network-participants.html) The Government of India reported **more than 116,000 live retail sellers across 630+ Indian cities and towns as of December 2025**. That is a national total; the cited figure is not broken out for Kerala and does not establish service coverage in any particular Kerala locality. [PIB, *Digital Blueprint for Ease of Doing Business*, March 2026, p. 5](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/mar/doc202636813601.pdf)

### DigiDukaan

ONDC describes DigiDukaan as a digitized procurement/replenishment initiative for kiranas, distributors, and brands. Its current public page reports **10K+ kiranas, 1K+ distributors, and 35+ brands**, and describes it as **live in Hyderabad**, with expansion to Jaipur and three other cities underway. These are figures and status claims published on ONDC’s page; the page does not identify Kerala among the named locations or give a Kerala retailer count. Do not present DigiDukaan as live in Kerala without a current city-level confirmation. [ONDC: DigiDukaan](https://www.ondc.org/pages/digidukaan.html)

### KiranaPro

KiranaPro’s website currently markets a neighborhood-shopping service as ONDC-powered. Its Google Play listing describes voice-led grocery ordering and nearby-store delivery, shows **10K+ downloads**, and lists **26 June 2025** as its last update at the time checked. These are public product-page claims and listing metadata; they are not independently verified activity or active-user figures. [KiranaPro website](https://www.kirana.pro/), [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kiranapro)

Google Play lists the developer as KIRANAPRO SOFTWARE PRIVATE LIMITED and gives a Thrissur, Kerala address. A listed address is not proof of service coverage. The public sources above do not establish current live-store coverage, completed order volume, or operational availability across Kerala. Treat those points as **unverified** until checked in the app for the target PIN code or confirmed directly with the company. Do not conflate KiranaPro with ONDC’s separate DigiDukaan initiative.

## Research limits and follow-up

- The Kerala DES market survey is titled 2021–22 and was published in March 2025; it is the latest directly relevant statewide establishment count used here. It should not be described as a current 2026 census of all shops.
- No shopkeeper interviews or local demand study are represented here. The figures do not validate Kannect’s problem or product assumptions.
- Before making a competitor-coverage claim, record the date, PIN code, app version, seller/store surfaced, and whether a test order can actually be placed. A company address, app listing, network-wide seller count, or launch announcement is insufficient by itself.
